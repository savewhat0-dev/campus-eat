Privacy Notice

Updated 3 November 2026

Who operates this service

BMU Eats is an independently operated campus project serving the BMU community. It does not claim university endorsement or ownership of users’ personal data. Project/privacy contact: rishabh.kaktan.25bb@bmu.edu.in.

Information used

University email and verification attempts are used to sign you in. Your name, enrollment/faculty ID, course/year and phone support your profile and pickup. Orders include selected items, quantities, pickup time, notes and order status. Retailer accounts, menu edits, uploaded photos and explicitly recorded counter receipts support outlet operations. Security/rate-limit records help prevent misuse.

How information is shared

The outlet fulfilling an order receives the associated pickup details. Other retailers are restricted to their own outlet’s records. Hosting/database providers process the service’s stored records. Google/Gmail processes sign-in emails. If enabled, Groq processes Chef message text and limited conversation search context; the service does not intentionally send your stored customer profile or order history to Chef. You may still disclose personal information in a message, so avoid including it.

Cookies and device storage

A first-party HttpOnly session cookie supports persistent sign-in, currently up to 30 days with renewal. Device storage saves preferences, bag, favorites, preview data and some entered pickup details. The Expo app keeps website cookies within its WebView. Signing out revokes the server session. Clear this website’s browser data, or the app’s storage, to remove device copies.

Purpose and choices

Information is used for sign-in, profiles, order fulfillment, order updates, outlet operations, support and abuse prevention. You can decline to submit optional notes or Chef messages. Required pickup information is needed to fulfill an order. No location, microphone or advertising permission is required for the app’s normal ordering flow. A retailer may choose a product photo to upload.

Retention and account requests

Data is retained while needed to provide the service, resolve order issues or meet applicable obligations. A single automatic deletion period has not been configured. Email rishabh.kaktan.25bb@bmu.edu.in to request access, correction, account closure, withdrawal of consent or deletion. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate obligations; the project team should explain any limits when responding. Removing consent for essential processing can prevent continued account/order use.

Safeguards and limits

The service uses HTTPS, expiring one-time sign-in links, protected session cookies, hashed retailer credentials, and outlet access checks. These measures reduce risk but are not a guarantee against every incident. Do not share sign-in links or retailer credentials. Access from a shared device requires care.

Questions and complaints

Start with the project/privacy contact above. Include your account email and request, without sending a password or verification token. Users retain any rights and complaint routes available under applicable law.
