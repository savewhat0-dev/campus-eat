## How to read this menu

Single numbers are prices in ₹. Size prices follow the order stated beneath each category. A dash means the source does not show that price. “Not legible” means the photograph does not support a reliable reading. Photos are a record of their printed menus, not a guarantee of current price or stock. GreeNox prices are the displayed starting prices; paid customizations may change the total. The site showed the campus store closed when inspected; that does not mean its products are permanently unavailable. Taxes, packing fees and offers should be confirmed by each retailer.

Descriptions under GreeNox summarize ingredients supplied by that outlet. Ingredients are not invented where a source gives only a product name. Dietary and allergen status should be confirmed by the outlet rather than inferred solely from a name.

Keep the original Campus Eats layout, colours, typography, navigation, and ordering flow that I liked. Refine spacing, alignment, readability, and consistency. Use Blinkit and Zomato as references for clear browsing, obvious actions, and polished food presentation; retain Campus Eats branding. Avoid excessive boxes, thick borders, nested cards, clutter, and unnecessary decoration. Use light separators and whitespace where possible.

2. Campus photograph and logo
Replace the burger photograph in the HOME-PAGE HERO with the uploaded campus photograph, in the same image area. Do not redesign the whole hero or replace food product images with the campus photo. Preserve the photo's proportions and keep the building recognisable across screen sizes.
Place the uploaded BMU logo ONLY inside the TOP-RIGHT corner of this campus photograph. Anchor it to the image container so it moves with the photograph. Remove duplicate placements of this university logo from the header, footer, and other sections; keep the separate Campus Eats brand. Preserve the complete logo and proportions, with comfortable inset spacing, approximately 16–24 px on desktop and 10–14 px on mobile. Use only a small, subtle light backing if needed for legibility. Never stretch it, cover important content, or let it extend outside the photo.

3. Update the website menu from the attached MD
Read the entire MD and reconcile the website catalog with it, including outlet names, categories, products, prices, sizes, variants, add-ons, and source notes. Do not merely upload the MD or add a download link.
Use these verified names: GreeNox — BML University, Maggi Point, Burger Singh, Café Coffee Day Xpress, and Domino’s Pizza. Do not invent missing ingredients, prices, dietary labels, ratings, or offers. Items without a confirmed price must not enter checkout with a guessed or zero price. Keep source conflicts in retailer/admin review rather than cluttering customer screens with technical notes.
Preserve stable product IDs and existing order history. Migrate catalog changes safely, revalidate active carts, and make search, Chef AI, cart totals, and retailer controls use the same catalog.

4. Product presentation and browsing
Show each product's name, relevant image, price or confirmation state, available variants, and clear Add/quantity controls. Use consistent image proportions without stretching. Reuse correct existing product assets; do not regenerate images already created. Create or request only missing assets, never use one dish's image for an unrelated product, and show an honest neutral fallback while an image is missing.
Keep search, outlet/category filters, favourites, cart, and empty/loading/error states clear and usable. Show dietary markers only when supported by the menu or retailer.

5. Chef AI and existing flows
Keep Chef AI conversational: reply naturally to greetings, ask a short question when a request is unclear, and suggest matching dishes for the user's request. For a named pizza or dish, show relevant products with their outlet, price, availability and correct image; avoid dumping the entire outlet directory.
Preserve student/faculty sign-in, retailer sign-in, pickup scheduling, required customer details, order status, and retailer operations. Do not add online payment. Keep existing booking rules and notices visible at the appropriate step without inventing charges or automatic deductions.

6. Motion, responsiveness, and verification
Use restrained 150–300 ms transitions for buttons, filters, quantities and panels, plus a light entrance effect where useful. Avoid blinking, continuous bouncing, excessive zoom, slow delays and distracting motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
Check small phones, tablets and desktop for logo placement, readable text, image cropping, touch targets, horizontal overflow, keyboard access, and dialog focus.
Verify the imported menu against the MD, correct variant/add-on totals, unavailable-item handling, search, Chef AI, cart, login and retailer controls. Preserve existing account security and site access. Finish with a concise change summary and clearly state any missing assets, unresolved menu conflicts, or checks that could not be completed.
