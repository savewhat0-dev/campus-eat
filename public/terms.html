About BMU Eats

BMU Eats is a campus food ordering project serving the BML Munjal University community. A university email helps establish community access. BMU Eats is independently operated unless and until the university confirms a formal relationship in writing. Use of the BMU name or logo does not by itself establish university ownership, endorsement or responsibility.

Access and acceptable use

Use your own @bmu.edu.in mailbox for student/faculty access. Retailer credentials are assigned for a specific outlet and must not be shared. Provide accurate pickup details and do not submit fraudulent orders, abuse other users or attempt to access another outlet’s records. If you require a parent’s or guardian’s consent to use this service, obtain it before providing personal information.

Menu, food and availability

Outlets control their listings and availability. Unknown or disputed prices must be confirmed before ordering. AI illustrations may differ from the food served. Dietary filters show only explicitly recorded menu labels; missing labels are not a safety guarantee. Confirm allergens, ingredients and cross-contact risks directly with the outlet before ordering.

Orders and pickup

An order request is subject to the outlet’s acceptance. Check its status and collect at the selected counter and pickup time. Preparation times are estimates. The outlet may reject an order and provide a reason. Cancellation and refund questions should be raised with the fulfilling outlet.

Payments

The current service does not collect online payments or automatically charge your account. Counter payment remains separate from order placement. Demo QR images do not collect money. Where an authorized outlet QR is provided, verify the payee and amount in your payment app. Opening or scanning a QR does not prove payment; only an explicitly recorded receipt is shown as received. BMU Eats does not automatically impose penalties or security-deposit deductions.

Chef AI

Chef assists with campus food discovery using the recorded menu. It may misunderstand a request. Confirm item details, availability, dietary needs and prices with the outlet. Avoid including passwords, financial details, health information or other sensitive personal details in Chef messages.
